            Topics

            Paste a list, or choose a file: an Anki deck (.apkg), an Anki text export, or a CSV/TXT/JSON word list (for example from Duoninja, Quizlet or a spreadsheet). For typed lists, put one word per line:

            korean, english, level, topics, Korean definition, example
사과, apple, 1, food, 빨갛고 둥근 과일, 아침에 {사과}를 먹었어요.
공항 - airport
환율	exchange rate	TOPIK 5	shopping
            Format details
            • Only the Korean word and English meaning are required. Commas, tabs, |, or word - meaning all work.
            • Level can be 1/2/3, 초급/중급/고급, A/B/C, or TOPIK 1–6.
            • Separate several topics with ;. New topic names become new topics.
            • Spaces inside a Korean word are removed (생일 파티 → 생일파티). Words can be up to 8 syllables; one-syllable words (책, 밥…) go to the 한 글자 game.
            • JSON: an array of objects like {"korean":"사과","english":"apple","level":1}.
            • Anki: choose the deck’s .apkg file (AnkiWeb download or File → Export → Anki Deck Package), or a Notes in Plain Text export. The app finds the Korean and English fields on each card and uses longer Korean fields as example sentences.
            • Duolingo / Duoninja: export your word list as CSV and choose it here. Header rows like Word, Translation are recognized.
            • Words that are already built in keep their level and clues. Cards with full sentences or one-syllable words are skipped.
